Chassis Operations Platform

A clear operating view of vehicle location, event history, transfers and dispatch documents, supported by guarded data synchronization.

Why the solution exists

Location and movement context were distributed across sources. The solution gives operations a lookup surface while separating ingestion, change planning, event history and documents into distinct components.
